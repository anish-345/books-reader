## 🎯 Two-Step Process

### Step 1: Take Real Screenshots (5 minutes)
1. Open your Book Reader app on Android
2. Take 5 screenshots (Power + Volume Down)
3. Transfer to PC
4. Save to `Desktop/app_screenshots/`

### Step 2: Enhance with Script (1 minute)

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Best Screenshots:
1. **Clean your app** - Remove test data
2. **Add real books** - Use actual PDFs/EPUBs
3. **Good lighting** - Bright screen
4. **No notifications** - Enable Do Not Disturb
5. **Portrait mode** - Vertical orientation

### For Best Enhancement:
1. **High resolution** - 1080x1920 or higher
2. **Clear content** - Readable text
3. **Good composition** - Centered UI
4. **No personal info** - Remove sensitive data
5. **Multiple shots** - Take extras, choose best

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Screenshot not found"
**Solution:** Make sure files are in `Desktop/app_screenshots/` with correct names

### "Enhancement looks wrong"
**Solution:** Check screenshot quality - should be high resolution and clear

### "Colors look off"
**Solution:** Adjust PRIMARY_COLOR and ACCENT_COLOR in script

### "Text not readable"
**Solution:** Use screenshots with clear, readable content
